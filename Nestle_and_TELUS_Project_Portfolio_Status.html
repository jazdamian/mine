2. Discovery Workshop

TELUS: Supporting
Purpose
Market shares project information. Define store visit process, business context, expected outcomes, reporting needs and TIR scope.
Session Owner
Market
TELUS Participation
Ask questions as needed. Not expected to present or moderate topics.
Prerequisites
None
1A Day In A Life
  • Step by step description of the store visit by the Sales Rep / Merchandiser who will use TELUS Image Recognition.
2Situation at the Store
  • How does the shelf usually look?
  • Known issues.
3Business Objectives
  • What business value is TIR intended to deliver for the market?
4Expected Outcomes
  • What will be done with the data provided by the TIR system?
  • Expected benefit for Sales Rep / Merchandiser, Field Manager and other teams.
5Reporting Requirements
  • Which KPIs are tracked via Power BI reports?
  • Which reports are in use today and what additional reporting is needed?
6Scope for TIR
  • Number of products and categories.
  • Approach to competitor products.
