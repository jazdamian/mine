Where Nestlé Brazil decides

three approvals

Each approval releases the next block of work. A late approval moves everything after it by the same number of days.

Approval 1 · after the workshops

Business rules and recognition approach

Confirms how images are recognised: facings, facing groups, edge cases per category, and the agreed limitations. Releases both the Blueprint and the catalogue analysis.

Approval 2 · stage 3

Product catalogue analysis

Nestlé reviews the SKU-level findings, answers open questions and confirms the catalogue is correct for training.

Approval 3 · stage 2

Blueprint

Sign-off on the full solution document, TELUS and SSBS sections combined. This is the last approval before scope freeze.

Open dependency

affects the schedule above

On-shelf photo quality

SSBS has reviewed the on-shelf photos supplied for Brazil and found quality problems: blurred images, glare, and shots taken at an angle rather than square to the shelf at the required distance. SSBS is preparing a findings pack with examples.

If a new or supplementary photo set is required, the field capture sits outside this plan and adds time before the 25 September catalogue approval can be given. The volume affected will be known when the findings pack is shared.

Next step: review the findings pack with Nestlé Brazil, agree the photo standard, and confirm whether a re-capture is needed. Decide this before the workshops so the schedule reflects it.
